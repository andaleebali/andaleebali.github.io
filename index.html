About Me

I am a Geospatial Analyst with over a year of experience and a Master’s in Earth Observation and Geoinformation Management from the University of Edinburgh. Before transitioning into GIS, I studied Mathematics and spent nearly four years as a Systems Engineer.

Currently I'm working as a Spatial Developer at Doncaster City Council, where I build tools and automate workflows that help teams make better decisions with spatial data. I’m particularly interested in applying geospatial technology and remote sensing to environmental management, conservation, infrastructure planning, and public service projects that create real-world impact.
